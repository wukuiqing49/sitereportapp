Photo evidence and location records: what it does in SiteReport

Capture photos for inspection items and associate them with notes, time, and location information. Learn the exact problem, options, verified steps, and known limitations.

Publisher 上海促动科技有限公司

On this page

What this feature helps you doWhere it fits in the workflowWhat to check before exportWhat to keep in mind

A photo without its inspection context is difficult to use as evidence later.

What this feature helps you do

  • Capture one or more photos for an inspection item
  • Associate photos with checklist records
  • Record location and capture time when available

Where it fits in the workflow

  1. Open an inspection item
  2. Capture or select the evidence photo
  3. Review the photo and continue the inspection

What to check before export

  • Photo source, inspection item, and visible evidence annotations

What to keep in mind

  • Location details depend on device location availability and permission.
