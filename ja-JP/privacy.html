プライバシー

完全なプライバシーポリシーは以下に英語で掲載しています。日本語でのお問い合わせや削除依頼は wukuiqing@gmail.com までお送りください。

1. Who we are and scope

SiteReport (Android package com.wkq.site) is provided by 上海促动科技有限公司 under the developer name AndroidManTou. Contact: wukuiqing@gmail.com. This policy explains how the Android app and this product website handle information. It takes effect on 16 October 2026.

2. Inspection content and permissions

Projects, checklists, notes, selected or captured photos, annotations, signatures, branding, templates and generated reports are stored on your device for inspection work. Camera access captures photos; location access adds coordinates to evidence when you use location features. Photos and reports can contain location, time, names, signatures and other personal or client information. Review them before sharing. You can revoke camera, location or notification permissions in Android settings; related features may then be unavailable.

The release app uses Google Play services or Android system location services. It does not request background-location permission. Notifications and foreground data-sync services support report delivery and its progress. Core inspection content is not sent to our purchase-verification service as part of its analytics.

3. Sign-in and purchases

Optional Google Sign-In provides an account identifier, email, display name, profile image and authentication token for sign-in and account display. Google handles authentication. The app can associate the signed-in user identifier with Firebase analytics. Google Play handles payment; our app does not collect payment-card details.

Our developer-operated DevHub service processes purchase tokens, product or subscription type, verification results and a random installation identifier to verify purchases, reconcile entitlements and prevent abuse. Its current default configuration does not forward your Google ID token for purchase-account linking.

4. Analytics, diagnostics and advertising

The release app enables Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage measurement, crash diagnosis and stability. These services can process app-instance identifiers, app interactions, device and operating-system information, crash traces, diagnostics and network information. DevHub separately receives first-open and basic activity events, manufacturer, model, Android version, language and country/region settings. Country/region settings are not GPS coordinates. DevHub custom inspection events and custom event properties are disabled.

The current release configuration disables AdMob advertising and advertising-ID collection. We do not describe this app as having no data collection: analytics, diagnostics, sign-in and purchase verification involve the processing described above.

5. Export, upload and recipients

When you share or export a report, the selected app, recipient or storage provider receives its contents. When you configure a delivery server, the app stores connection settings and credentials locally and sends selected reports to that destination. Enabling automatic upload or retry can cause later transfers without another tap. Disable automatic delivery and pending retries if you want to stop them. We do not control your chosen server or recipients; ask them separately to delete their copies.

Service providers include Google (sign-in, billing, location, analytics and diagnostics), our DevHub backend and its hosting infrastructure, and Cloudflare for this website and hosted infrastructure. They process data for the relevant services; Google also provides its own privacy terms. Information may be disclosed where required by law, to protect rights or security, or in a business transfer subject to applicable requirements.

6. Website storage and third-party links

This website remembers your selected language in browser local storage. You can remove it through browser site-data controls. Hosting providers process connection information such as IP address, request time and browser details to deliver and protect the website. Following Google Play, YouTube or other external links sends you to services governed by their policies. The video link does not load a YouTube player automatically.

7. Security and international processing

The app uses Android app-private storage and permission controls for app-managed data. DevHub requests use HTTPS. Delivery-server security depends on your selected protocol and configuration: unencrypted connections can expose files or credentials, so use encrypted transport and trusted recipients. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Providers and recipients may process information outside your country; applicable safeguards depend on the service and law.

8. Retention, local cleanup and server deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app. The in-app account-removal action clears the local account association, inspection database, app-managed report files, templates, signature assets and delivery settings. It does not delete files already exported to shared storage, sent to another app or uploaded to a server. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for app-managed data.

Purchase-verification records are retained as needed for entitlement restoration, reconciliation, security, fraud prevention, disputes and legal obligations. Analytics and diagnostic retention is governed by the relevant service configuration and provider policies. To request deletion of developer-held server data, email wukuiqing@gmail.com with the subject “SiteReport data deletion” and enough account or purchase information to locate the records. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or full card details. We may verify ownership; records that must be retained for the stated purposes may be restricted or de-identified. Local account removal does not automatically erase server purchase records, delete your Google account or cancel a Google Play subscription; manage subscriptions in Google Play.

9. Your choices and children

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, a copy of your information, restriction or objection to processing, or withdrawal of consent where consent applies. Contact the email above; you may also contact your local data-protection authority. Google account, payment and provider-held data can require a separate request to Google. SiteReport is intended for professional inspection work and is not directed to children under 13. If a child has supplied personal information, contact us so it can be investigated and removed where appropriate.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its revision date when data practices change, with additional notice or consent where required. Privacy and deletion requests: wukuiqing@gmail.com. Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Cloudflare privacy information: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.
