# Field notes and sign-off: what it does in SiteReport

Inspection findings and sign-off details can be lost when they are recorded outside the report workflow.

## What this feature helps you do

- Record inspection results, issues, and recommendations
- Manage signature profiles for field roles
- Include notes and sign-off information in generated reports

## Where it fits in the workflow

- Open the relevant inspection item
- Record findings and corrective recommendations
- Add or select sign-off details before report generation

## What to check before export

- Inspector, safety officer, supervisor, and company signature details

## What to keep in mind

- The available signature fields and report presentation depend on the selected report template.
