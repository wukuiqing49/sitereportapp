# Write inspection findings and review sign-off before reporting

Inspection findings and sign-off details can be lost when they are recorded outside the report workflow.

## What this feature helps you do

- Record inspection results, issues, and recommendations
- Manage signature profiles for field roles
- Include notes and sign-off information in generated reports

## Where it fits in the workflow

- Open the relevant inspection item
- Record findings and corrective recommendations
- Add or select sign-off details before report generation

## What to check before export

- Inspector, safety officer, supervisor, and company signature details

## What to keep in mind

- The available signature fields and report presentation depend on the selected report template.

## Separate the observation from the recommendation

Describe what you observed, where it occurred, and which evidence supports it. Keep a corrective recommendation distinct from the observed condition. For example, identify a damaged item and its location before stating that it should be repaired. Avoid recording an assumed cause as an established fact.

Before generating the report, review the selected signature profiles and the people or roles they represent. Check the output itself: signature fields and their presentation depend on the chosen template. A signature image in a report does not by itself establish a particular legal approval process.
