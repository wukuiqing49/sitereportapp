Common questions

Does this provide a certified construction checklist?

No. SiteReport provides a configurable inspection workflow and does not claim regulatory certification, legal compliance, or a universal construction standard.

Can construction site visits include photos?

Yes. The photo evidence workflow links captured or selected photos to inspection records and items.
