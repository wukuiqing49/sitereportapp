Verified feature

Photo evidence and location records

Capture photos for inspection items and associate them with notes, time, and location information.

The problem it solves: A photo without its inspection context is difficult to use as evidence later.

What you can do

  • Capture one or more photos for an inspection item
  • Associate photos with checklist records
  • Record location and capture time when available

Available options

  • Photo source, inspection item, and visible evidence annotations

Supported inputs

  • Camera photos, gallery photos, inspection notes, and location permission

Outputs

  • Organized photo evidence linked to an inspection record

Workflow

  1. Open an inspection item
  2. Capture or select the evidence photo
  3. Review the photo and continue the inspection

Limitations

  • Location details depend on device location availability and permission.

Common questions

Can photos be linked to an inspection item?

Yes. The photo workflow associates captured evidence with the current inspection record and item.
