Common questions

What should an inspection checklist contain?

The right points depend on the site and workflow. SiteReport supports checklist items, inspection plans, notes, photos, and report outputs without presenting a universal regulatory template.

Can an inspection checklist be reused?

Yes. SiteReport supports reusable inspection plans and checklist templates for repeated field work.
